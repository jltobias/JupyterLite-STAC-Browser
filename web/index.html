02 / EXPLORE THE FOOTPRINTS

Coverage, in context.

Study areaCollection coverageItem footprints
Edit polygon coordinates

One pair per line. Move, add, or remove vertices by editing the lines. Apply converts the area to a polygon and closes its ring automatically.

Drag the purple area to move it; drag corners to resize. In a polygon, click midpoint handles to add vertices and right-click a vertex to remove it. Use Edit polygon coordinates for keyboard or touch editing. Pause Edit study area to select item footprints. Cancel drawing or Escape keeps the previous area.

Footprints show metadata coverage, not population raster values. No raster data is downloaded.
